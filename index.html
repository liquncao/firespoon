Company

Good service deserves
better tools.

A customer’s question is often the start of a sale. Yet answering it can mean searching a catalogue, checking stock and finding the right colleague. At Firespoon, we build software to make those connections easier, starting with the building supplies trade.

Built around the enquiry

A product code, a rough measurement or “the same as last time”. Customers arrive with different starting points. Our software is designed to help them take the next step.

Clear at every step

We favour familiar language and focused choices, so customers can ask for what they need without learning a new way to shop.

Room for judgement

Some questions need an experienced salesperson. We design for that handover, so the conversation can continue with the right person.
